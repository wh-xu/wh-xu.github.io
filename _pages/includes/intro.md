Weihong Xu (徐炜鸿) is currently a ZJU100 Young Professor in the College of Integrated Circuits, [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/). 
Prior to joining ZJU, he was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [Embedded Systems Laboratory (ESL), EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/esl), working with [Prof. David Atienza](https://people.epfl.ch/david.atienza). He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from [UC San Diego (UCSD)](https://cse.ucsd.edu/), where he was advised by [Prof. Tajana Rosing](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~trosing/).

His research focuses on next-generation computing architectures for efficient and reliable AI systems, including: 
a) 🚀 RISC-V-based AI chip, b) computer architecture and EDA co-design, c) near-data computing, d) LLM and AI acceleration. 

He received B.E. and M.E. degrees from [Southeast University](https://www.seu.edu.cn/english), under the supervision of [Prof. Chuan Zhang](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Chuan_Zhang24). He also completed a research internship at [Intel Labs China](http://intel.com), where he developed a Flexible MIMO Processor for beyond-5G communication systems.
